VACATINA CANONICAL LOGO IMPLEMENTATION RULE

Use ONLY the official assets in this pack.

1. Never redraw, regenerate, approximate, trace, recolor, crop through, stretch, mirror or reconstruct the Vacatina logo.
2. Never remove the tagline from any supplied full-logo asset. The tagline “we treasure your holidays” remains part of the supplied official full-logo lockup.
3. Do not create a wordmark-only or no-tagline full logo unless Vacatina supplies that derivative as an officially approved asset.
4. When space is constrained, switch to the supplied standalone official Vacatina mark instead of altering the full logo.
5. Default corporate full logo: Vacatina_Logo_Primary_FullColor.png.
6. Default compact digital mark: Vacatina_Mark_SpaceCadet.png.
7. Use approved reverse artwork on dark backgrounds; never manufacture inverse variants with CSS filters.
8. Preserve official colors exactly:
   Space Cadet #1B1464 / RGB 27,20,100
   French Blue #0071BC / RGB 0,113,188
   White #FFFFFF / RGB 255,255,255
   Black #000000 / RGB 0,0,0
9. Maintain official clear space and original proportions.
10. Audit desktop header, mobile header, mobile navigation, footer, favicon, PWA/app icons, social profile/share assets, auth surfaces, loading/error states, and remove every generic or synthetic Vacatina logo implementation.
