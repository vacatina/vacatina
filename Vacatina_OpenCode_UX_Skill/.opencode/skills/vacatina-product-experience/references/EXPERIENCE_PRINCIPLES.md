# Vacatina Creative UX Principles and Quality Bar

## Design vocabulary — original, premium, grounded
- **Search Command Center**: a confident, low-cognitive-load input area. The existing 11-tab homepage SCC is locked; extend its visual language inside product pages.
- **Decision canvas**: results designed for choosing, not just browsing. A tight hierarchy: route/offer identity → time/duration/stops → fare family → luggage/rules → verified total → action.
- **Fare confidence**: informative transparent states for price freshness, changes, missing inventory and checked availability. Prices must be source-backed.
- **Trip dossier**: a persistent coherent place for meaningful selected travel components, with editability, recoverability and actual readiness statuses; not a fake cart checkout.
- **Assisted commerce**: a premium real route for enquiry/quotation when live supplier order is unavailable. Request action must truly work, or use verified phone/email/contact links.

## Creative exploration without endless planning
Design three *plausible* product concepts, select one, implement. Evaluate all by: clarity, decision quality, travel-specific trust, accessibility, responsiveness, component reuse, and cost to integrate. Prefer product experience innovations over ornamental styling.

## 10 high-value travel-specific patterns
1. Editable persistent search summary that never wipes valid inputs.
2. Multi-city as editable ordered segments, not a cloned one-way form.
3. Price-date strip only when backed by actual available prices; else calendar editing without invented values.
4. Compact cards with progressive disclosure for long itineraries and fare terms.
5. Side-by-side fare family selection on wide screens; accessible stacked comparison on phones.
6. Distinguish included bag, paid bag, carry-on and passenger-specific baggage rules.
7. Airline-supported seats/extras only; no pretend seat inventory.
8. Transparent pricing breakdown with verified currency and total; show uncertainty when not revalidated.
9. Save, inspect, edit and re-check selections; mark changed and expired offers explicitly.
10. Strong accessible mobile selection CTA with clear next step and no obstructive floating overlays.

## Motion / visual craft
- Use transitions to explain changes of context, not for spectacle. Animations should feel precise and effortless; apply reduced-motion preferences.
- Consistent density system: generous premium whitespace in headers, efficient information density in results, forgiving touch spacing in forms.
- Use semantic content hierarchy and layout grids, avoid custom icon-font hacks and massive hero imagery that hides search tasks.
- No invented trust badges, reviews, airline partnerships, rates, price guarantees, availability or countdowns.
- Respect color, font, spacing and geometry tokens derived from CURRENT approved Vacatina design. When a reference disagrees with canonical code, do not globally overwrite the design system.

## QA: demonstrate rather than declare
For each changed screen, show: baseline → updated screenshot; route reachable; primary CTA actual outcome; error/empty/loading; keyboard + mobile; responsive width; content accuracy. Browser tests that visit routes alone do not demonstrate a working journey.
